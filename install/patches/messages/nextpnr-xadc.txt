xilinx: XADC

Pack XADC onto XADC_XADC, place it on its single site, return a constant
tie on DCLK/CONVSTCLK to GND, and write INIT_40..INIT_5F.  The rule is
only made when the design has an XADC.  Needs a chipdb made with
nextpnr-xilinx-meta's site_type_XADC.json and the prjxray-db
MONITOR_BOT pseudo-pips and INIT features.
