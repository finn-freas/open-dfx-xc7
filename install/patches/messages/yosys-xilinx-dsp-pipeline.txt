xilinx_dsp: -pipeline (synth_xilinx -dsp_pipeline)

Under -pipeline, for xc7:
- input registers of one DSP A or B input that are split over several
  register cells with the same clock and enable are merged (a reset
  that not all bits share is moved into D), so A2REG/B2REG are packed;
- an A/B cascade may pass through two registers (ACASCREG/BCASCREG=2);
- a reset-free register after a fabric $add tree of DSP P outputs is
  moved back onto those outputs and packed into PREG where PCOUT is
  unused.
Without the option the pass takes the old path.
