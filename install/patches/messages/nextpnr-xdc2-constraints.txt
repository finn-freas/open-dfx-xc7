xilinx, timing: ASYNC_REG, I/O delays, IOB, path exceptions, uncertainty

- ASYNC_REG flip-flop chains are clustered in one slice.
- set_input_delay/set_output_delay time the port paths, with the clock
  delay from its pad and pad/BUFGCTRL delays the chipdb lacks.
- IOB TRUE moves a register into the ILOGIC IFF or OLOGIC OUTFF.
- set_false_path/set_multicycle_path -from/-through/-to,
  set_max_delay [-datapath_only] and set_min_delay are applied as tagged
  clock domains, with Vivado's precedence.
- Hold is checked between related clocks; set_clock_uncertainty and
  input/system jitter enter the requirement.
- Vivado's rule_gclkio_bufg clock-placement check (Place 30-574);
  CLOCK_DEDICATED_ROUTE FALSE turns it into a warning.

A design without these constraints has no tagged domain and no
ASYNC_REG/IOB cells, and is placed and routed as before.
