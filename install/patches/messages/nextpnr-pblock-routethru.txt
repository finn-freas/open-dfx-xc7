xilinx: no LUT route-through in a routing-contained pblock for a net that leaves it

A LUT route-through in a logic tile of a pblock that contains its
routing belongs to the pblock's own logic: a later module built for the
same pblock may place a cell on that LUT.  Only a net whose driver and
users are all members of the pblock may take one; checkPipAvailForNet
refuses it for any other net.  No-op without pblocks.
