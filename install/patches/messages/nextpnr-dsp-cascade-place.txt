xilinx: place a DSP48E1 cascade along the chipdb wires (-o dsp-cascade-place)

walk_dsp gives each cascaded DSP a fixed offset of 5 rows per DSP tile,
but the step is 6 rows across an HCLK or BRKH row, so a PCOUT -> PCIN
chain longer than one clock region had no legal placement.  The RAMB36
cascade walk is made generic (cascade_next) and a DSP48E1 cluster
follows the PCOUT0 -> PCIN0 wires when -o dsp-cascade-place is given.
Off by default.
