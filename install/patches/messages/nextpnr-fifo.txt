xilinx: FIFO18E1 and FIFO36E1

FIFO18E1 is placed on the FIFO18E1 bel of the lower RAMB18 site and
FIFO36E1 on the RAMBFIFO36E1 bel, with Vivado's pin map, and their FASM
is written (mode, widths, registers, inverters, offsets as Vivado stores
them, FIFO_PORT_WIDTH).  ECC, a non-zero INIT/SRVAL and FIFO36E1
DATA_WIDTH 4 are refused.  Needs the prjxray-db FIFO_PORT_WIDTH
features.
