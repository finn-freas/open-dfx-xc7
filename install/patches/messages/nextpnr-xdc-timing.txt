xilinx, timing: read the XDC timing commands Vivado designs use

XDC reader: object queries take {a b c} lists, globs, -hier, -filter on
NAME, -quiet and -include_generated_clocks; Vivado register names
(cnt_reg[3], u_a/q_reg) resolve to the flip-flop that drives the net; a
backslash continues a line; set_property takes nets and pins (with a
warning), PULLUP/PULLDOWN/KEEPER TRUE, a site-local BEL and a repeated
value; create_clock takes -name and -waveform; create_generated_clock,
set_clock_groups and set_false_path between clocks or on cells of one
side are applied after packing.  Every command or property that is
accepted but not applied gets a "NOT applied" warning.

Timer: a clock constraint knows its name, its primary clock and its
first edge, so clocks of one primary (MMCM/PLL outputs, generated
clocks, BUFR dividers) are checked against their edge relationship
instead of only being listed as cross-domain.  Clock pairs in a false
path get no criticality and no report.  Criticality of other paths is
unchanged, so a design without the new commands places and routes as
before.
